About Horus

A reliable travel partner in Iraq

Horus is among Iraq’s leading airline agents, issuing tickets on all international airlines through multiple reservation systems. Large companies, international organizations and individual travellers trust us with every part of their journey.

Our clients pay zero commission: we are paid by the airline, not by you. We also regularly price tickets below the fare shown in the booking system. It narrows our margin, and we do it anyway, because long-term partnerships matter more to us than any single booking.

Behind every booking is a customer-oriented team of qualified, knowledgeable travel specialists, backed by modern reservation systems, careful trip-data management and partners worldwide.

“Integrity, business excellence and good service are the hallmark of our agency.”
  • IntegrityTransparent pricing with no service fees on changes or refunds.
  • ExcellenceEvery option on every airline, compared for you.
  • ServiceFriendly advice from the first quote to your safe return.

Why Horus

Our commitments to every client

Simple promises we keep on every booking, whether it’s one ticket or a hundred.

  1. 01

    A reply within 15 minutes

    Send us a route and we’ll come back fast with every available option across all airlines.

  2. 02

    Zero commission

    Our commission comes from the airline, never from you.

  3. 03

    Free ticket changes

    We charge nothing to change a ticket. You only pay the airline’s fee, if there is one.

  4. 04

    Free refunds

    Refunds carry no charge from us. Only the airline’s own fee applies, if any.

  5. 05

    Fares below the system price

    We regularly offer tickets for less than the price shown in the booking system.

  6. 06

    We own our mistakes

    If we make an error issuing a ticket, or fail to update you on schedule changes or travel requirements, we take responsibility.
